Importing photos

You can import photos from your library directly inside Keepsake, or select photos in the Photos app and use the Share Sheet to send them straight to Keepsake — on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Free vs. Pro

Keepsake is free to try with up to 7 photos. Keepsake Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited photos and every backdrop — there are no subscriptions.

If you've already purchased Pro and it's not showing as unlocked, try Restore Purchases from the upgrade screen inside the app.

Recording issues

Keepsake needs camera and microphone access to record. If recording isn't working, check that both are enabled for Keepsake in Settings → Privacy & Security on your device.

Exporting

Exported videos are saved to your Photos library (iPhone/iPad) or to a location you choose (Mac). Keep the app open and your device unlocked while an export is in progress.

Something else?
