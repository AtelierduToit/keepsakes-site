What you get

More than a slideshow

🎙️

Narrate as you show

Your face appears in the corner while your photos fill the frame. Swipe through each image at your own pace and speak naturally — just like being there in person.

🎬

Export for everywhere

Publish in 9:16 for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts — or 16:9 for presentations, YouTube, and family screens. Export both at once with one tap.

iPhone, iPad & Mac

Shoot on your phone and edit on your Mac, or do everything in one place. The same project flows across all your devices through your Photos library.

Draw as you speak

On iPad, use Apple Pencil to annotate directly on photos while recording. Every stroke is timed to your narration and appears in the final export exactly as you drew it.

Choose from ten backdrop styles — solid black, crisp white, warm paper, textured grid, and more. The background frames your photos without competing with them.

Remote control on iPhone

Recording on Mac? Use your iPhone as a wireless remote to navigate slides and trigger recording. No cables, no extra apps — just connect over your local network.

Location & weather captured automatically

When you start recording, Keepsake quietly notes where you are and what the weather is like. That context appears as a card in the exported video — date, city, temperature.

No account. No login. No tracking. Keepsake has no servers and collects nothing about you — your photos, recordings, and videos never leave your device. There is nothing stored that could be used to identify or target you.

For photographers

Your craft deserves
the context it was made in

A great photo is never just the image — it's the lens, the light, the moment. Keepsake surfaces the technical and human story behind every frame so your audience understands not just what they're looking at, but how it was made and where you were when you made it.

EXIF metadata

Camera body, lens, aperture, shutter speed, and ISO are read from each photo and can be composited directly into the export.

Location & weather

City, country, temperature, and weather condition are captured at recording time and displayed in a clean info card on the video.

9:16 and 16:9 in one go

Post the vertical cut to Reels and the widescreen cut to your blog or portfolio — both generated from the single recording session.

Your voice, not captions

The narration is the story. People hear your judgment and intention as they see the image — a dimension no caption can match.

Getting started

From photo library to shareable video in minutes

1

Import your photos

Pick photos from your library, drag them in on Mac, or paste them from anywhere. Keepsake sorts them by date and you can reorder them before you record.

2

Hit record and start talking

Your front camera opens in the corner of the screen while your first photo fills the frame. Speak naturally — no script needed, no takes. Record, pause, and keep going.

3

Swipe through your story

Tap or swipe to move to the next photo while you keep recording. Each slide change is tracked so the export transitions exactly in sync with what you said.

4

Export and share

Choose 9:16, 16:9, or both. Keepsake composites your narration, photos, camera feed, annotations, and metadata into a single polished video — ready to save to Photos or share directly.
